KISANGA-Q Data Story

Where are Quilombola communities in Brazil?

This story uses official data to show how Quilombola communities and inhabitants are distributed across Brazilian states and biomes.

How to read this map

Darker states have higher values for the selected metric. Use the menus to switch between the number of Quilombola communities and the number of Quilombola inhabitants.

Limitations

  • Counts depend on the completeness and update frequency of official data sources.
  • State-level aggregation may hide important differences within each state.
  • Biome-level summaries depend on the territorial classification available in the input data.
  • The map shows distribution, not causal relationships.
